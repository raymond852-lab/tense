Answer Key — For Parent Only

DO NOT GIVE TO STUDENT BEFORE TEST
A. Tom's Diary (8): (1) runs — He/She/It + -s, every day = Now. (2) plays — same. (3) am — I am, today = Now. (4) was — Yesterday = Before, I was. (5) went — Yesterday = Past, go → went. (6) had — Past, have → had. (7) visited — Past, + ed. (8) did not — did not + base stay.
B. Can / Could (4): (1) could not / couldn't — When I was five = Before. (2) can — Now. (3) were — lots of people = many. (4) was — a big ball = one. Full sentence for (5)(6) in question: Could I catch it? No, I couldn't — this tests Yes/No. Accept: could, couldn't.
C. Conjunctions (6): 1. in order to (purpose). 2. so (result). 3. Even though (contrast). 4. because (why). 5. but (different). 6. thus (result).
Common trap: while / since are not correct here.
D. Reflexive (6): 1. himself — brother = He, 1. 2. yourselves — Students = You, many. 3. themselves — girls = They, many. 4. yourself — Mary = You, 1. 5. ourselves — We, many. 6. himself — Tom = He, 1.
Spelling: themselves (not theirselves), yourselves (not yoursleves).
E1. Word Order (3): 1. Where did you buy the storybooks? 2. Mum was very happy. 3. There was a cake on the table.
E2. Yes / No (3): 1. Yes, I did. 2. No, I wasn't. / No, I was not. 3. Yes, he could.
Must be full short answer, not just Yes / No.
F. Verb Table (10): 1. bought, buying. 2. caught, catching. 3. said, saying. 4. knew, knowing. 5. copied, copying — y → i + ed, -ing keeps y.
G. Writing (14): Check with 3 questions: Yesterday? All past? Helper? Base? Could once? Ending? Was ___ happy?
Model: Yesterday was a sunny day. I wanted to go to the park to buy sandwiches. I knew Mum liked sandwiches, so I wanted to choose sandwiches. I was so happy. Then I said to Mum, “Sandwiches, please!” Then Mum said, “Ten dollars!” All of a sudden, I did not have any food. I was shocked and looked at Mum. In the end, I saw my friend Mary. She could help me because she had food. We ate sandwiches together and Mum was very happy.
Marks: Content 5 (4 pics + ending), Language 5 (Past + Helper + Spelling), Organisation 2 (4 paras), Key Elements 2 (could once + conjunction once).
