Present Participle (~ing) vs Present Continuous

Present Participle (~ing) = just the ing word. playing, running, taking. It is a word form.

Present Continuous = am / is / are + Present Participle. He is playing now. They are going now.

Part A: write only the ing word. Part C / D / G: write the full tense.

Key words: every day / every week / every morning / every summer = Simple Present. now / at the moment / at present / Look! / Listen! = Present Continuous.

How to decide

Simple Present: I / You / We / They + base verb | He / She / It + verb + s / es / ies
He plays every day. They play every day.

Present Continuous: I + am + ing | He / She / It + is + ing | You / We / They + are + ing
He is playing now. They are playing now.
