3 Golden Rules

1⏰

Time Word = Tense

80 years ago → past tense

Nowadays → present tense

2🔄

Past vs Present Verbs

Past: was, were, had, used, washed

Present: is, are, has, use, wash

3 Sentence Patterns

Most Important!
PATTERN 1

There was/were (Old) vs There is/are (Now)

80 years ago, there was/were ___. Nowadays, there is/are ___.
  • ✔80 years ago, there were steam trains. Nowadays, there are MTR trains.
  • ✔In the old days, there weren't any mobile phones. Nowadays, there are many mobile phones.
  • ✔100 years ago, there was no airport. Now, there is an airport.
PATTERN 2

People + past verb vs People + present verb

In the old days, people + past verb. Nowadays, people + present verb.
  • ✔In the old days, people washed clothes by hand. Nowadays, people wash clothes with a washing machine.
  • ✔80 years ago, people used fans to keep cool. Now, people use air-conditioners.
  • ✔People used a Walkman to listen to music. Nowadays, people use phones.
PATTERN 3

Nobody / Everybody

Nobody / Everybody + past. Everybody + present.
  • ✔100 years ago, nobody had a mobile phone. Nowadays, everybody has one.
  • ✔In the old days, everybody used a fan. Nowadays, everybody uses an air-con.
📝

Model Paragraph — For Exam!

4 sentences = full marks
In the old days, 80 years ago, there were old steam trains in Kowloon City and people washed clothes by hand. There weren't any mobile phones and nobody had a computer. Nowadays, there are MTR trains and there are many mobile phones. Everybody has a computer and people wash clothes with a washing machine.
Orange = Old Days Green = Nowadays
⚠️

Common Mistakes — Red Alert!

Mistake 1: Old days use weren't NOT aren't (Q40)
❌ Wrong
80 years ago, there aren't any phones.
✅ Right
80 years ago, there weren't any phones.
💡 Tip: aren't = present, weren't = past. 80 years ago = past!
Mistake 2: Mirror Rule for short answers Q37,39 (Was → was, Were → were)
❌ Wrong
Was there a school? Yes, there were.
✅ Right
Was there a school? Yes, there was.
💡 Tip: Question and answer must mirror: Was → was, Were → were.
